void scripting::ScriptRuntime::retire ( std::unique_ptr< ScriptRuntime > runtime)
staticnoexcept

Destroys a runtime that may still be executing.

A runtime replaced from inside one of its own calls would be freed under its own stack frames. It waits in a list instead, and drainRetired() destroys it once nothing runs in it any more
