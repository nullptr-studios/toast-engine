A trace means "any text that should be logged". There can be multiple importances of traces but anything that is not a really important message falls into this category

If you are logging something and you don't know if reading this log will be meaningful in half an hour, or even a week, then it is a trace

If you will stop caring about this log as soon as you finish working on your task, then it is a trace
