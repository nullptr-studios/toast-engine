Detailed Description

Dispatch table for one protobuf-backed event type over the FFI.

Populated by registerProtoEvent<T>() and stored in the proto registry. The three function pointers cover the three directions of the FFI bridge: sending from C#, subscribing from C#, and cancelling a subscription.
