Detailed Description

Voxel shapes built from an ordered list of volumes.

Author
Xein
Date
26 Sep 2026

A ProceduralVoxel rebuilds its shape from its children, top to bottom Groups are walked depth first and disabled children are skipped

Every piece draws into its own grid with its first script's editShape The grid is cached so moving, rotating or reordering pieces never runs a script again
